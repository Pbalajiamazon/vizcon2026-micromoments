# Data Sources — Micro-Moments of Happiness

## Primary Sources

1. **ATUS Well-Being Module (Bureau of Labor Statistics)**
   - Activity-level happiness, stress, tiredness, sadness, and meaning scores
   - Fielded 2010, 2012, 2013, and 2021
   - https://www.bls.gov/tus/modules/wbdatafiles.htm

2. **Kahneman Day Reconstruction Method (DRM) Research**
   - Original research on experienced well-being during daily activities
   - Kahneman et al. (2004), Science, 306, 1776-1780
   - https://www.researchgate.net/publication/279235616

3. **OECD Time Use & Well-Being Data**
   - Cross-country time use with well-being indicators
   - https://data-explorer.oecd.org/

4. **World Happiness Report 2026**
   - Country-level happiness rankings
   - https://www.worldhappiness.report/ed/2026/

5. **FlowingData: Happiness and Meaning by Activity (2023)**
   - Visualization reference using ATUS Well-Being Module
   - https://flowingdata.com/projects/2023/happy-meaning-acts/

6. **OECD Screen Time and Subjective Well-Being (2025)**
   - Digital vs. physical activity well-being comparisons
   - https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/screen-time-and-subjective-well-being_c840403c-en.html

## Data Notes
- Activity-level happiness scores are based on the 0-6 ATUS scale, normalized to 1-7 for readability
- Cross-country activity happiness estimates synthesized from OECD/DRM comparative studies
- Demographic breakdowns from ATUS microdata (US-representative)
- Daily participation rates from ATUS and OECD time use surveys
- All sources publicly accessible and free
